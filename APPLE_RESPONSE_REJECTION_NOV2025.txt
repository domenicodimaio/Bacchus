GUIDELINE 4.0 - DESIGN (iPad UI) - RESOLVED

Issue: Text overlapped in the Subscriptions screen on iPad Air (5th generation).

Resolution: We have completely redesigned the subscription screen with proper iPad support:

1. Fixed iPad Detection: Implemented robust device detection using multiple methods (Device.modelName, Platform.isPad, screen dimensions)
2. Optimized Layout: 
   - Reduced card padding and margins for better fit
   - Changed text alignment from center to left for better readability
   - Removed fixed heights to allow dynamic content sizing
   - Added ScrollView to prevent content overflow
3. Tested on Multiple iPad Models: iPad Mini, iPad Air, iPad Pro 11", iPad Pro 12.9", iPad (9th gen)

The subscription screen now displays perfectly on all iPad models with no text overlap.

Build: 2914 (submitted today)
