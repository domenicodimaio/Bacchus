• Progressive absorption: considers alcohol isn't absorbed instantly
• Food factor: slows absorption, reducing peak BAC

4. SCIENTIFIC ALGORITHM (WIDMARK FORMULA)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BASE FORMULA:
BAC = (A / (r × W)) - (β × t)

Where:
• A = grams of pure alcohol consumed
• r = Widmark distribution factor (0.68 males, 0.55 females)
• W = body weight in kg
• β = metabolism rate (0.15 g/L/hour)
• t = elapsed time in hours

SCIENTIFIC REFERENCES:
✓ Formula developed by Erik Widmark (1932)
✓ International standard for forensic calculations
✓ Used by NHTSA (National Highway Traffic Safety Administration)
✓ Used by forensic scientists in court
✓ More accurate than consumer breathalyzers (50-74% false negatives - BMJ Open 2014)

WHY IT'S RELIABLE:
"The Widmark Formula is the same method used by police and courts to estimate 
BAC in the absence of blood tests. I'm not inventing anything, I'm using 
standard forensic science."

• Emphasize that safety is your priority

• Listen carefully to their concerns
• Take detailed notes
• Ask clarifying questions

• Explain educational approach

• Ask for reference examples
• Confirm you understood what they want

• Summarize key points discussed
• Confirm next steps
• Ask about timeline
• Thank them again
