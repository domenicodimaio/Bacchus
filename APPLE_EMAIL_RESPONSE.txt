You specifically mentioned that the Register button was not visible on iPad Air (5th generation) running iPadOS 26.0.1. We have completely resolved this issue along with other potential iPad compatibility problems.

- All interactive elements now meet or exceed Apple's 44x44pt minimum touch target guidelines

3. Responsive Design Improvements:
- Implemented proper content constraints to prevent UI crowding on larger screens

- Enhanced visual hierarchy that remains clear at larger scales

4. Typography and Spacing:
- Increased minimum font sizes to 16pt for interactive elements
- Improved line spacing and margins for better readability on iPad
- Enhanced contrast and visual clarity for upscaled interface elements

5. Component-Level Optimizations:
- Fixed Footer components: Added minimum height constraints for all buttons
- Session management buttons: Optimized sizing and spacing for iPad use
- Form elements: Improved keyboard handling and content visibility
- All modal dialogs: Enhanced sizing for upscaled environments

TESTING VERIFICATION:

We have thoroughly tested the updated app on iPad Air (5th generation) with iPadOS 26.0.1 and confirmed:
