GUIDELINE 1.4 - PHYSICAL HARM (BAC Calculator)

We understand and deeply respect Apple's commitment to user safety. However, we would like to respectfully challenge the assertion that software-based BAC calculators are inherently less accurate than hardware devices.

We have attached scientific documentation demonstrating that well-implemented software-based BAC calculators using established algorithms (such as the Widmark formula) can actually be more accurate than many consumer-grade breathalyzers, particularly inexpensive hardware devices that may lack proper calibration or maintenance.

Our app is designed as an educational tool with the following comprehensive safety measures:

- Multiple prominent disclaimers on every screen with BAC calculations
- Clear warnings that calculations are for educational purposes only
- Explicit statements that the app does not replace professional medical devices
- Strong advisories against making driving decisions based on app calculations
- Warnings about legal limitations and individual variations
- Modal disclaimers that users must acknowledge before accessing calculations

The app promotes responsible drinking awareness and helps users understand alcohol metabolism concepts through education. It provides general information based on scientifically established formulas, not medical advice.

We believe that with our enhanced disclaimers, educational focus, and the scientific evidence we have provided, our app serves a valuable public safety purpose by promoting alcohol awareness and responsible consumption habits. We respectfully request reconsideration of this guideline application.

GUIDELINE 4.0 - DESIGN (Sign in with Apple)

We have identified and resolved the issue where our app was requesting name/email information after Apple Sign In.

We have modified the authentication flow to automatically use the name and email provided by Apple Sign In. Users who sign in with Apple now see their name pre-filled in the profile setup, eliminating duplicate requests for information already provided by Apple's Authentication Services framework.

The app now follows Apple's Sign in with Apple design guidelines completely and provides the consistent experience users expect.
