## 🎓 APP PURPOSE (FOR REVIEWERS)

**Bacchus is an educational tool that:**

1. ✅ Helps users understand how alcohol affects the human body
2. ✅ Provides scientific information about alcohol metabolism using the Widmark formula
3. ✅ Educates users about legal BAC limits and consequences
4. ✅ Encourages responsible drinking through awareness
5. ✅ Does NOT suggest fitness for any activity or replace medical devices

**What Bacchus is NOT:**

1. ❌ NOT a medical device or breathalyzer replacement
2. ❌ NOT a decision-making tool for fitness assessments
3. ❌ NOT intended to determine ability to perform any activity
4. ❌ NOT a substitute for professional medical testing
5. ❌ NOT claiming any accuracy for real-world decision-making
